### Summary

- **Major:** Updated Dockerfiles and Helm chart to version 1.2.2-rc.700 and 1.2.2-rc.683 for consistency across services, enhancing farmer registration metadata.
- Refactored SQL metadata for farmer registration, improving organization and validation rules, including updates to personal information and household sections.
- Enhanced farmer registration process with new features: added disability support, fallback middle names, and improved poverty score computation.
- Improved user experience by moving socio-economy to its own tab and adjusting tab section orders for better organization.
- Implemented automatic removal of disability table records when disability status changes, and ensured either birth_date or estimated_age is required for registration.
- Addressed multiple translation errors across different pages and made minor bug fixes to enhance overall functionality.
